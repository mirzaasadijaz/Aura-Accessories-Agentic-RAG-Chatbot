The Aura Accessories - Official Company Knowledge Base
Last Updated: September 2026

1. ABOUT US
The Aura Accessories is a premium online boutique specializing in luxury fashion items. Our collections feature exclusively curated handbags, elegant footwear, precision-crafted watches, and fine jewelry designed to elevate everyday style.

2. SHIPPING POLICY
- Standard Shipping: Deliveries take 3-5 business days. 
- Express Shipping: Next-day delivery is available for orders placed before 2:00 PM.
- Free Shipping: We offer complimentary standard shipping on all orders exceeding $150.

3. RETURN & EXCHANGE POLICY
We accept returns within 14 days of delivery. Items must be in their original, unworn condition with all tags and luxury packaging intact. Watches and jewelry must include the original certificate of authenticity. 
Note: Custom-engraved jewelry and sale items are final sale and cannot be returned.

4. PRODUCT CARE INSTRUCTIONS
- Leather Handbags: Keep away from direct moisture. Use our complimentary Aura Leather Conditioner once a month.
- Watches: Our luxury watches are water-resistant up to 50 meters, but leather straps should not be submerged.
